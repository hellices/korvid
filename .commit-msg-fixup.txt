test: narrow the eval provider to LiteLLMProvider for mypy

`mypy` covers `tests/` as well as `src/`, and `provider_factory_from_env`
is typed as returning the `LLMProvider` ABC — which has no `_plan`. The
new parity and CLI tests read the plan to prove the eval and the app
agree on it, so they need the concrete type.

Both files now narrow once, in a helper, with `isinstance`. That removes
the `attr-defined` errors and makes the shipped-transport claim an
assertion in every test that inspects a plan rather than an implicit
assumption.
